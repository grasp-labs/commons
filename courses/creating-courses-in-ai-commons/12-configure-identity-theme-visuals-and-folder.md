## Course identity

- **Course name** is the gallery title and the starting point for the folder slug.
- **Description** is the one-line promise shown on the gallery card.
- **Public** is available only to the platform tenant. Turn it on when the course should be available in every tenant.
- **Cover image** identifies the course in the gallery.

Keep the name concrete and the description focused on what the learner will be able to do.

## Theme and background

Open **Course settings** beside the course name.

1. Choose a family: AIC, Light, Nature, Color, or Dark.
2. Compare the available templates and select one for the whole course.
3. Or upload a background image: it replaces the template (**Use uploaded image**), and removing it returns to AIC Signature.

**Cover vs background**

- Cover: the gallery card.
- Background: every course slide.

Prefer one quiet visual system. A strong background must never reduce text contrast.

## Folder

AIC generates the folder from the course name. Click the folder value in **Course settings** to edit it.

Change it when:
- the generated name is too long;
- the course needs a stable abbreviation;
- your team uses a clear naming convention.

Keep it short, lowercase, and meaningful. Do not add the public or tenant prefix yourself — AIC manages it when visibility changes.

> Rename through the editor so paths and metadata remain synchronized.
