## From idea to a published learning path

A strong AIC course is **not a document split into pages**. It is a guided sequence in which every step has one clear job.

### You will learn how to

- define a useful learner outcome before writing;
- structure steps and columns without overcrowding the slide;
- write readable Markdown with links, tables, code, and images;
- turn Mermaid source into clear diagrams;
- choose a theme, review the result, and publish safely.

> **Rule of thumb:** one step, one idea, one useful action for the learner.

At the end, you will have a repeatable workflow for building courses that are easy to scan, understand, and maintain.
