---
title: Final quality check before publishing
---
## Content

- [ ] The course promises one clear learner outcome.
- [ ] Every step has a descriptive title and one primary idea.
- [ ] Instructions use concrete verbs and realistic examples.
- [ ] Dense material has been split instead of squeezed onto one slide.
- [ ] Spelling, terminology, links, and code samples are correct.

## Visuals and accessibility

- [ ] The cover still works when cropped in the course gallery.
- [ ] Heading levels create a logical hierarchy.
- [ ] Images have meaningful alt text or adjacent explanation.
- [ ] Mermaid diagrams render without fallback errors.
- [ ] Columns feel balanced at the normal application width.
- [ ] The course remains readable in light and dark mode.

## Delivery

- [ ] Preview navigation works from the first step to the last.
- [ ] PDF export has been checked when offline reading matters.
- [ ] View and Edit access match the intended reviewers.
- [ ] Draft, published, tenant, and public states are intentional.

> **Done means the learner can act without asking the author what the slide was supposed to mean.**
