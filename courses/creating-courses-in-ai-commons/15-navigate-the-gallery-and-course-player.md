## Course gallery

- **Search** filters by course name, folder, description, and step titles.
- **Refresh** requests the latest course list.
- Switch between **grid** and **list** without changing the data.
- **New course** starts a fresh draft.
- Each card shows Active or Draft and Public or Private.
- A **NEW** badge highlights a published course added since the learner’s last course check.

## Course player

- Previous and Next move through the learning path.
- The center counter shows progress.
- Download PDF creates an offline copy.
- Owners also see Share, Delete, and Edit course; on the platform tenant, also the Public switch.
- Edit opens the currently viewed step, so revision starts in context.

A learner without Edit access should see the course content and navigation — never the management actions.
