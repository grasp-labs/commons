## Playbooks: teach the system your way of working

Commons encodes know-how in two ways:

| | Skill | Playbook |
|---|---|---|
| Where it comes from | captured automatically from a run that worked | written by a person |
| What it says | *what to run* | *how to approach it* |
| Value on day one | none — nothing captured yet | immediate |

On day one there are no skills, because nothing has been done yet. A written playbook steers the system from the very first attempt — and those good first attempts are what get captured as skills, offered to everyone once an admin promotes them.

> **So playbooks are how you start the flywheel instead of waiting for it.**

Good ones are specific. "Always check X before Y, because Z fails silently" is worth more than three pages of principles.
