## Welcome: make AI actually land in your team

You have been asked to be an **AI Champion**. Not to build the platform — to make it useful where you actually work.

### You will learn how to

- explain Commons to colleagues in their own language;
- find what already exists before creating anything new;
- capture the knowledge that currently lives only in people's heads;
- write a playbook so the system works your team's way;
- answer the safety questions you will certainly be asked;
- choose and run a first use case that sticks.

> **Rule of thumb:** you are not selling a tool. You are removing a specific annoyance for a specific person.

No coding is required at any point in this course.

> **Who this is for:** team leads, project managers, and subject-matter experts who want to help their team actually use AI Commons — no engineering background needed.  •  **Time to complete:** ~120 minutes.
