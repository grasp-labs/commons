## Turn tribal knowledge into cited knowledge

Commons stores knowledge as a graph: items (**nodes**), the links between them (**edges**), and the **evidence** behind them. Everything has a real address, so it can be cited rather than copy-pasted.

One design choice is what makes this trustworthy: **the evidence travels with the claim.** You can always follow an assertion back to where it came from. Items also carry a confidence score and a trust level for their source, set by whoever records them — so a sourced fact and somebody's guess never look identical.

> **Try it now:** write down one piece of team knowledge and record where it came from. Refuse to save it without a source. Notice how much harder that is — and how much better the result.
