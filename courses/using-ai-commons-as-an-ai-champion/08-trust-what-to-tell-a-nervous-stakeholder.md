## Trust: what to tell a nervous stakeholder

You will be asked whether this is safe. Four things are true, and worth knowing almost word for word.

- **It reaches only what you reach — nothing more.** Access follows your identity, and every sensitive action is **logged and audited**, so you can always see what was done, by whom, and on whose behalf.

**Risky actions stop.** Ordinary work runs. Sensitive actions pause for confirmation. The heaviest wait for explicit approval before anything happens.

**Secrets go in, not out.** Keys live in a vault, and their values are never returned by any read, to anyone.

**Numbers are computed, not recalled.** Every figure comes from a real, read-only query on your actual data, returned with how it was produced — never a model remembering a figure. That is the substantive answer to "doesn't AI just make things up?"

> Do not oversell. A Champion who overpromises does more damage than no Champion at all.
