## The Champion is a translator, not an engineer

Engineers know what the platform can do. Your colleagues know what the work actually needs. Almost nothing useful happens until someone speaks both languages.

That someone is you — and you already hold the part that is hardest to get:

- the spreadsheet nobody admits to maintaining;
- the approval that always stalls in the same place;
- the question three people get asked every week;
- the exception to the policy that was never written down.

None of that is visible to an AI until a person puts it somewhere the system can reach.

> **Your job is not to be the person who uses Commons. It is to be the reason your team does.**
