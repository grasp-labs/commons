## What Commons is, in one sentence

**Commons gives people — and the AI agents they work with — one consistent way to reach your organisation's own knowledge, files, memory, secrets and computing power.**

Underneath sit eleven services: access, configuration, files, data lake, secrets, memory, knowledge, two for incoming data and two for AI. You never need to memorise them. They share one surface, so whatever you learn in one place transfers to the next.

Commons is also built on open, widely used standards rather than a private interface. In business terms: **when a better model arrives, it can be connected without a rebuild.**

> That last line is your answer to "aren't we backing the wrong horse?"
