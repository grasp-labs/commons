## Search before you create

Five tools do nearly all of the looking:

- **`ls`** — browse what is in a place, like opening a folder
- **`find`** — filter by type, owner, date or status
- **`grep`** — search inside content by keyword
- **`cat`** — read one thing exactly as it is
- **`info`** — read it *with* its context and its sources

This is the single most important habit you can model. Duplicated, half-right knowledge is how a system like this rots. Once two documents disagree, people stop trusting all of them — and trust does not come back easily.

> **Try it now:** find three things in your Commons you did not know existed, and one that is already out of date. Decide who owns fixing it.
