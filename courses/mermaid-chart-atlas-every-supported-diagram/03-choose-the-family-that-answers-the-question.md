title: Choose the family that answers the question
---
## Flow and behavior

Use these when the question is **what happens and in what order?**

- Flowchart and swimlane
- Sequence
- State
- Journey
- Event modeling
- Git graph
- Gantt and timeline

They make transitions, messages, ownership, or time visible.

<!-- ds-course-column:2 type=markdown -->

## Structure and architecture

Use these when the question is **what exists and how is it organized?**

- Class and ER
- Mind map and tree view
- C4 views
- Architecture and block
- Requirement
- Packet
- Railroad grammars

They expose boundaries, hierarchy, contracts, and composition.

<!-- ds-course-column:3 type=markdown -->

## Data and strategy

Use these when the question is **how do values or options compare?**

- Pie and XY
- Radar and quadrant
- Sankey and treemap
- Venn
- Wardley
- Cynefin
- Ishikawa
- Kanban

They emphasize magnitude, overlap, position, uncertainty, or cause.
