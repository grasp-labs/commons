# A visual language for complex ideas

Mermaid turns compact text into diagrams that stay close to the knowledge they explain. In AI Commons, a fenced `mermaid` block becomes an interactive visualization with zoom, fit, fullscreen, and source access.

## This atlas contains

- **44 tested Mermaid examples** covering every supported family and important variant;
- a focused explanation of when and why to use each chart;
- a dedicated **source → rendered result** slide immediately after every explanation;
- the core syntax elements and practical readability checks.

## How to use the course

Read each pair together: first understand the communication pattern, then inspect the full source beside its rendered output. Change one line at a time when adapting an example.

> Choose the diagram from the question you need to answer — not from the shape you want to draw.
