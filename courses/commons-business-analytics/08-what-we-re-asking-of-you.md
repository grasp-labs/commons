## What we're asking of you

You do not need to become the Commons expert. We are asking for:

- one candidate report or dataset from the BI team to pilot *(see Step 7)*;
- a look at the catalog before rebuilding anything that already exists;
- your feedback on where Pipelines / Clone / Workflow fit your week.

> **Rule of thumb:** your job is to be the reason BI trusts its data — the platform does the moving.
