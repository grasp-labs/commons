## Try it: a first pilot

The fastest way to make this real is one small, real job:

1. Pick one recurring report or dataset that is painful to keep current.
2. Choose the block: **Pipeline** to land it on the lake, **Clone** to copy it out, or a
   **Workflow** to chain the steps.
3. Wire it through Commons once, together.
4. Measure the time saved versus the manual way.

> **Rule of thumb:** one dataset, one block, one measured win — then expand.
