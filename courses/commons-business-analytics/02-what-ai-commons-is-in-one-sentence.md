## What AI Commons is, in one sentence

**AI Commons gives people — and the AI they work with — one consistent, governed
surface over the organisation's knowledge, files, state, secrets, configuration
and compute.** It is built on open protocols and made by Aider.

For a BI team, the practical version:
- one governed place to find and reach data, instead of scattered extracts;
- the same permissions everywhere — the number you report is the number others see;
- a foundation the compute blocks build on to move and deliver that data.

> **Rule of thumb:** if you have ever asked "which extract is the real one?", that is the problem Commons removes.
