## The layer upstream of your reports

You already turn data into decisions in Power BI and SQL. **AI Commons** is the
governed layer *upstream* of that work — where data is reached, moved, and made
trustworthy before it lands in a report.

### You will learn
- what AI Commons is, in one sentence;
- the eleven services it gives you to work with;
- the three compute blocks — **Pipelines**, **Clone**, and **Workflow** — and when to use each;
- how it fits the Power BI and SQL you already run.

> **Rule of thumb:** this is not a new BI tool. It is what feeds and governs the data your BI tool depends on.

> **Who this is for:** BI analysts, data engineers, and report owners who already work in Power BI and SQL.  •  **Time to complete:** ~60 minutes.
