# Welcome to AI Commons

Commons is our platform for **data, AI and governance**. Tenants connect their
systems, land data in their own datalake, run compute on it, and work with it
through AI agents that use governed tools.

**The platform in one line**

| 1 | 11 | 3 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| IdP | APIs | compute blocks | tools |

By the end of this course you will know how to log in, what the pieces are,
and where to go to build things.
