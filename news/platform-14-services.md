# The platform map now covers 14 services

AI Commons now documents 14 focused platform services behind one identity,
tenant, IAM, and audit model. The expanded map adds one-time capability sharing
and governed outbound channels to the existing data, workflow, event, AI, and
developer-tooling planes.

The two additions are explicitly marked as previews. The public service guide
states their boundaries and maturity so teams can plan against facts rather
than roadmap shorthand.

The same platform map drives contracts, documentation, knowledge-graph
evidence, and release checks in Commons Control.
